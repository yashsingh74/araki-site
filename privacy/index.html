Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Araki makes and sells handmade candles. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The short version

  • This website doesn’t have accounts and doesn’t store your details on any server.
  • When you order, your details go straight from your device to us on WhatsApp, and only when you press send.
  • We only use your details to make, deliver and support your order. We never sell them.
  • No advertising trackers, no analytics and no cookies on this website.

What we collect

  • Order details you send us: your name, mobile number, delivery address or pickup choice, the items you ordered and any note.
  • Payment reference, if you share it (for example a UPI transaction ID), so we can match your payment.

We never see or collect your card number, bank details or UPI PIN. UPI payments happen inside your own UPI app and bank.

Stored on your device only

Your shopping bag is saved in your browser so it’s still there when you come back. If you tick “Remember my details” at checkout, those details are saved in your browser too. Nothing is sent to us until you send your order, and you can clear this at any time by unticking the box or clearing your browser data.

How we use your information

  • To confirm, make and deliver your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To handle cancellations, refunds and replacements.
  • To keep the records that tax and accounting laws require.

Who we share it with

  • Delivery partners: your name, phone number and address, so they can deliver your parcel.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram (Meta), when you choose to message us there. Their own privacy policies apply to those apps.
  • Banks and payment providers that process your payment.
  • Our website host, which may keep standard security logs such as IP addresses.

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to support your order, and afterwards only as long as the law requires (for example, for tax records). We delete chat history and other details we no longer need.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal information, or to withdraw consent for us to use it. To do this, or to raise a concern or grievance, contact us below. We’ll respond within 30 days.

Security

This website is served only over HTTPS, has no customer database, and uses a strict content security policy so other sites can’t load scripts into it.
