Candle safety

  • Never leave a burning candle unattended, and keep it away from children, pets, curtains and anything flammable.
  • Burn on a heat-safe surface. Glass and tins become hot.
  • Trim the wick to about 5 mm before lighting, and stop burning when about 1 cm of wax is left.
  • Sculpted candles without a container should be placed on a heat-safe plate.

We aren’t responsible for damage caused by not following these instructions.
